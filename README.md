## 👋 About Me

I am a **Software Engineering student** at Shifa Tameer-e-Millat University who likes turning ideas into working things: AI tools, web apps, IoT systems and clean visual design.

Outside the code, I lead teams. I manage projects at **NextaSol**, head programme management at **YPDC**, and serve as **Lead Student Representative** of my department.

- 🤖 Fine-tuned an LLM into a supportive AI psychologist that knows when to refer a user to a real doctor

- 🎨 Former paid Graphic Designer, comfortable in Photoshop, Illustrator and Canva
